Business

Harbor Manufacturing continues to produce industrial water pumps and replacement valves. During the quarter, it began a pilot distribution program for commercial buildings.

Management discussion and analysis

For the three months ended December 31, 2025, pump revenue declined because municipal customers delayed replacement orders. Higher selling prices only partly offset lower shipment volumes.

Production costs decreased during the quarter because copper prices fell. Higher freight expenses partly offset the lower material costs.

Operating cash flow improved during the quarter because the company sold inventory accumulated in the preceding year.

The largest distributor represented 27 percent of quarterly revenue for the three months ended December 31, 2025. Annual and quarterly percentages cover different periods.

At December 31, 2025, the company employed 600 people. Factory operations employed 455 people.

Risk factors

A single supplier continues to provide cast housings for the largest pump model. Qualification of an additional supplier has begun but is not complete.

Flooding near the eastern factory could interrupt production and delay customer deliveries. This risk was added during the quarter.

Product defects remain a risk that could cause warranty claims and equipment recalls. No product recall occurred during the quarter.
