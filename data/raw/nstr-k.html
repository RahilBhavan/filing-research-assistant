Business

Northstar Software sells annual subscriptions to document workflow software. Its customers are small businesses and regional accounting firms.

Subscription contracts generally last one year. Customers pay in advance, and the company recognizes subscription revenue over the contract term.

Customer renewal decisions depend on product reliability, integration costs and available competing products.

Management discussion and analysis

For the year ended December 31, 2025, subscription revenue grew because existing customers purchased additional user seats. New customer sales also contributed to revenue growth.

Hosting costs increased during 2025 because customers stored more documents. The company also incurred migration expenses while moving workloads to a new cloud region.

Operating cash flow improved during 2025 because more customers paid annual subscriptions in advance. This improvement was partly offset by higher employee compensation payments.

The ten largest customers represented 18 percent of annual revenue in 2025. No individual customer represented more than 5 percent of annual revenue.

At December 31, 2025, the company employed 240 people. The engineering team employed 90 people.

Risk factors

The company relies on a single cloud hosting provider. A prolonged service outage could interrupt customer access and cause customers to cancel subscriptions.

Unauthorized access to customer documents could expose sensitive information. A security breach could cause remediation costs and damage customer trust.

Competitors may reduce subscription prices or bundle similar products with other services. Pricing pressure could reduce the company's profit margins.
