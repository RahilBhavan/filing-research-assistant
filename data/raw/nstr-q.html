Business

Northstar Software continues to sell subscriptions to document workflow software. During the quarter, it introduced an optional review queue for accounting firms.

Management discussion and analysis

For the three months ended March 31, 2026, subscription revenue grew primarily because of higher renewal prices. Sales of additional user seats contributed less than in the prior year.

Hosting costs decreased during the quarter because a cloud contract renegotiation lowered storage rates. Migration expenses ended in February 2026.

Operating cash flow declined during the quarter because several large customers shifted from annual advance payments to quarterly payments.

The ten largest customers represented 21 percent of quarterly revenue for the three months ended March 31, 2026. No individual customer represented more than 6 percent of quarterly revenue.

At March 31, 2026, the company employed 250 people. The engineering team employed 95 people.

Risk factors

The company continues to rely on a single cloud hosting provider. Service outages could interrupt customer access even after the storage contract renegotiation.

New export restrictions on document processing services could limit sales in certain markets. This risk was added during the quarter.

Unauthorized access to customer documents remains a risk. The company did not report a material security incident during the quarter.
